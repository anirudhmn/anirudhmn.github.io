Pixel Cricket privacy policy

Updated October 9, 2026

Pixel Cricket is operated by Anirudh Natarajan. For privacy questions or requests, contact anirudh.j.12@gmail.com.

Your game data

Club names, player customisation, career progress and game settings are saved locally on your device. The game does not require a game account, and we do not send your names, club names or saved games to our gameplay analytics service.

Optional gameplay statistics

If you choose to share gameplay statistics, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand play time, tutorial use, matches and seasons and improve the game. Collection stays off until you opt in. The game works if you decline.

Google may process gameplay interactions, an app-installation identifier, device and app information, approximate location, purchase activity and SDK diagnostic information. An installation identifier can link events from the same installation; these reports are not described as anonymous. We do not use these statistics for ad personalisation.

Analytics data is processed by Google and may be processed outside your country. See Google’s privacy policyand Firebase privacy information.

Your choices and requests

To stop sharing, open Settings → Gameplay Privacy → Stop Sharing Stats. This stops future collection, discards unsent play time and resets analytics data on your device. It does not delete previously collected reports held by Google.

To ask about access, deletion or other privacy rights available in your location, contact the email above. We may need additional information to identify a relevant installation; we do not have a named game account linking your identity to analytics reports.

Purchases and advertising

Apple handles in-app purchase transactions. The app checks purchase entitlements to provide purchased features. We do not receive your payment card details. Google Analytics may measure purchase activity when statistics sharing is enabled.

Pixel Cricket uses Google AdMob for ads at eligible match breaks and optional ads that offer in-game rewards. Premium removes forced match ads; optional rewarded ads remain available.

When ads can be requested, Google’s advertising and consent services may process your IP address and approximate location, device and app identifiers, ad views and clicks, other app interactions, crash logs, performance information and your privacy choices. This data supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service reliability. Non-personalised ads still involve data processing.

The app requests non-personalised ads and disables Google’s publisher first-party ad identifier. It does not request permission to access Apple’s advertising identifier. Apple’s privacy-preserving ad attribution mechanisms may still be used.

Google’s consent service checks whether a privacy message is needed before ads are requested. Where required, you can revisit your choices in Settings → Ad Privacy → Ad Privacy Options. Ad privacy choices are separate from optional gameplay statistics: declining gameplay statistics does not turn off advertising data processing.

Google and participating advertising providers may process data outside your country. See Google’s advertising privacy information. Ad data retention depends on the provider and processing purpose. Contact us about applicable access, deletion or other privacy requests.

Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Analytics retention depends on the Google Analytics project settings; aggregate reports may be retained longer. Contact us with questions about retention or deletion.

Support email

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to answer your request and keep it as needed to resolve the issue. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above.
