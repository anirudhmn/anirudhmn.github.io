Anirudh Natarajan Games Support Privacy

Pixel Cricket support

For help with Pixel Cricket, email anirudh.j.12@gmail.com.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and a short description of what happened. Screenshots can help; avoid including personal or payment information.

Purchases

Use the restore purchases option in the game with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Apple manages App Store payments and refunds; you can request a refund at Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Saved games

Career progress is stored on your device. Deleting a save or removing the app can erase progress.

Gameplay privacy

You can change optional gameplay statistics sharing in Settings → Gameplay Privacy. See the privacy policyfor details.
